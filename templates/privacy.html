Photos we take

Our photographers take photos in public areas of Big Sky Resort and at sessions you book. Photos are posted in our online gallery, organized by date, location and (for portraits) the last name you give the photographer, so you can find and buy them. Galleries are public: anyone browsing a date and location can see the watermarked previews.

To help you find your photos, we label each photo with the sport (ski or snowboard) and jacket and pants colors, using our own software. We do not use facial recognition.

Photos stay in the online gallery for about one year. We keep an archive after that.

Want a photo taken down? Use our photo removal form or email __BIZ_EMAIL__ with the date, location and photo number (shown under each photo) and we'll remove it from the gallery. Parents and guardians can ask us to remove photos of their children at any time.

What we collect

  • When you buy online: your name and email, plus a billing and shipping address if you order prints or frames. Payment is completed on our shop (bigskyphotos.com) and processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.
  • When you buy in our store: your name and email (to send your receipt and download link), and your phone number or address if you give them. Card payments go through Square; we don't store card numbers.
  • Saved photos: if you save your photo selection, we keep your first name, email address and the photos you chose, so you can load them again and so we can help you if you call us. We may email you a reminder about your saved photos, and every reminder includes a link to unsubscribe. Saved selections are deleted once you order, or at the end of the season.
  • When you browse: our server keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and to keep the site running. Your cart is saved in your own browser so it's there when you come back.

How we use it

  • To deliver your photos, prints and frames, send receipts and download links, and answer your questions.
  • To send occasional follow-ups, such as a request for a review or an offer. Every one has an unsubscribe link, and you can also reply or email us to stop them. Receipts and download links are always sent.
  • To keep records our business needs (sales, taxes, refunds).

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies or tracking pixels on this site. Your cart is kept in your browser's own storage, and staff use a sign-in cookie for the studio. Our shop at bigskyphotos.com, where you pay, may use cookies needed for checkout.
